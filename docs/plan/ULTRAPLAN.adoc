= Canonry: plan to v1.0.0
:revdate: 2026-11-15
:toc:

Canonry is the Agda theory for the first tier of proof for Chapel array
programs. This plan is the execution layer. The milestones M0 to M4, the
theorem names and the refuted loop draft stay in
link:../status/ROADMAP.adoc[the roadmap]. This file adds the v1.0.0 gates, the
order, the models and the effort.

Canonry and `hyperpolymath/chancel` are a pair. Canonry holds the theory.
Chancel is the planned Chapel front end. The two follow one schedule.

The work that is shared with the sibling verifiers (`atoma`, `quintessence`,
`Apodeixis.jl`, `bactrian`, `chancel`) is in the family plan,
`docs/plan/FAMILY-ULTRAPLAN.adoc` in `hyperpolymath/atoma`. IDs F1 to F12 and
waves W0 to W5 come from that plan.

== Definition of v1.0.0

Canonry is a theory library. Proof Witness Grades apply to it. The seven
verifier gates of the family plan apply to `chancel`, not to Canonry.

[cols="1,4,3",options="header"]
|===
| Gate | Acceptance | Falsifier
| Theory scope | M1, M2 and M4 are proved: full-run safety, definedness conditions, loops with and without an invariant, symbolic bounds, nil, n-D domains, procedure boundaries. | A closed counterexample to a stated theorem checks in Agda.
| No escape | 0 postulates, 0 holes, `--safe`, in the whole import cone. | A postulate or a hole is in the cone.
| Recheck | Each stated theorem has a Rocq leg and a Lean leg with reconciled statements. | A leg proves a weaker statement.
| Receipts | A receipt from the pinned checker exists for the release commit. The grade in `PROOF-STATUS.adoc` is derived from it. | A grade is written with no receipt.
| Generator relation | The relation between `genVC` in the theory and the generator in `chancel` is stated and checked. | The generator emits a condition that the theory does not cover.
| Release and estate | Deed identity set. README and changelog true. | A claim names a property that the roadmap lists as not proved.
|===

Target grade at v1.0.0: `PWG-2ii` for each stated theorem. `PWG-1` needs
per-commit receipts from a pinned headless checker. Decide that target when
the estate checker is available to this repository.

== Use of the ECHIDNA coprocessor

Strided domains need exact `gcd` and `lcm` for alignment. A coprocessor can
search for counterexamples to a draft rule before a proof attempt. The refuted
loop draft shows the value of that step. The coprocessor has no external entry
point today (family plan, F10). Until it has one, use small closed
counterexamples in Agda, as the roadmap did.

== Stop points

* Do not restore the refuted loop draft.
* If a loop rule fails one of the two recorded counterexamples, stop and
  change the rule. Do not weaken the theorem.
* A leg that needs a postulate is not a leg. Record it as an open obligation.
